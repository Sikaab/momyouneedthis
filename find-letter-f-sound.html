🔊 Know The F Sound Before You Hunt

You are hunting the f sound: /f/, made with the top teeth resting on the bottom lip while air blows through. It is the sound at the start of fish, frog and flower — and also fox, flag, fan and fork.

Your mouth is the best detective tool on this hunt. Say each name slowly: if your top teeth land on your bottom lip and air starts to blow, the picture starts with f. Better still, f can be stretched — ffff — so you can hold the sound and check it before you commit to a circle.

After the hunt, keep the case going at home: f hides at the end of words too — leaf, giraffe and off. Finding those is the detective's bonus round.
