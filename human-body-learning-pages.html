🧠 Why Learn About The Human Body?

Learning about the human body helps children become curious about themselves and how they work. It can build vocabulary, observation skills and an early understanding of science while helping children appreciate everything their bodies can do.

👀 Start With Body Parts

Begin by learning the names of familiar body parts. Talk about what each part helps us do and encourage your child to point to them on themselves.

🎁 Explore Body Parts

💡 Let Your Child Discover Their Own Body

Learning about the human body can happen during everyday moments. Talk about your child's hands while washing them, their legs while running or their eyes while looking at something interesting. Simple conversations can turn everyday experiences into science learning.
