🔍 Shape Counting Game

Turn learning into a game! Scatter shape cutouts or toys on the floor and ask your child to count how many circles, squares, and triangles they can find. Point and count slowly together — one, two, three!

💡 Why Count Shapes?

Counting shapes teaches two skills at once: number sense and shape recognition. Children practice touching each shape as they count, which builds the careful one-to-one counting habit that early math depends on.
