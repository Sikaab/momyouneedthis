💡 Why Count Shapes?

Counting shapes teaches two skills at once: number sense and shape recognition. Children practice touching each shape as they count, which builds the careful one-to-one counting habit that early math depends on.

What do children learn from counting shapes?

Counting shapes builds one-to-one correspondence, number recognition, and shape identification all at once. Children learn to touch each shape as they count and connect the final number to the whole group.
