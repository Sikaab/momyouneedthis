🧠 Why Learn About Space?

Space naturally encourages children to wonder, ask questions and explore new ideas. Learning about planets, stars and astronauts can help build curiosity while introducing simple early science concepts.

🪐 Start With The Planets

Begin by exploring the planets in our solar system. Talk about what makes planets different and encourage your child to notice their colors, sizes and positions.

🎁 Explore The Planets

💡 Let Your Child Be Curious

You don't need to know every answer. Ask questions like "What do you think is on that planet?" or "Why do you think astronauts need a spacesuit?" Let your child's questions lead the conversation.
