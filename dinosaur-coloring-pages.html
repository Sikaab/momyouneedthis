Meet the dinos

🦕 Meet the Dinosaurs on This Page

These dinosaur coloring pages feature four famous dinosaurs, and each one has something fun to color. The T-Rex was a meat-eater with huge teeth and tiny arms — color the teeth white and the body green or brown, and leave the little arms a lighter shade so they stand out.

The Triceratops had three horns and a big frill behind its head. It ate plants, so give it a leafy green or dusty brown body, and color each horn a pale yellow. The Brontosaurus had a very long neck and a very long tail — try a soft green body with a darker stripe down the neck.

The Stegosaurus is easy to spot: plates along its back and a spiky tail. Color each plate a different shade — one row light, one row dark — and the tail spikes a sandy yellow. Naming each dino out loud while coloring turns these dinosaur coloring pages into a first dinosaur lesson.

Real colors

🎨 What Colors Were Dinosaurs Really?

Scientists think most dinosaurs were green, brown, or grey — colors that helped them hide in forests and plains. But some may have had feathers and bright colors, like red crests or blue tails. Nobody knows for sure, which means there is no wrong answer on these dinosaur coloring pages.

Let your child pick: a pink T-Rex, a purple Triceratops, a rainbow Stegosaurus — all fine. If they want realistic colors, suggest green, brown, and grey with a darker belly. If they want wild colors, hand over every crayon in the box.

Two more ideas after coloring. First, a dino stomp parade: hold the finished page up high, stomp around the room, and roar like the dino on the page. Second, a fossil dig: bury toy dinos in a bin of rice or sand and dig them out with a spoon, just like a paleontologist.
