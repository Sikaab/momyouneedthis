❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What baby guides are available here?

This library covers baby sleep schedules, night waking, naps, bedtime routines, simple daily routines and baby essentials. You'll also find free tools like a baby tracker and a sleep schedule generator.

How do I know if my baby's sleep schedule needs adjusting?

Our sleep schedule guides walk through typical wake windows and nap needs by age, so you can spot when bedtime is too early, too late or when a nap needs to drop.

Why does my baby keep waking up at night?

Night waking is one of the most common questions new moms ask. Our night waking guides explain common reasons babies wake and share practical ideas you can try tonight.

What do I actually need for a baby?

Less than most checklists suggest. Our baby essentials guide focuses on the basics that are genuinely useful and the extras you can comfortably skip.

Are the baby tools really free?

Yes — the baby tracker and the sleep schedule generator are free tools you can use right in your browser.
