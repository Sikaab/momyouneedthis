Step by step

✏️ How To Use This Printable

  1. Download and print the tracing page — cardstock holds up best for little hands.
  2. Trace the first line together, showing your child where to start and which way to go.
  3. Let them trace on their own with a pencil, crayon, or washable marker.
  4. Celebrate and repeat — print fresh copies anytime for extra practice.
