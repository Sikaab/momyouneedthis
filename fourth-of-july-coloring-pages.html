What Is Independence Day? (For Little Kids)

July 4th is the birthday of the United States. Long ago, the country decided to become its own country, and Americans have celebrated that day ever since — the holiday's real name is Independence Day.

That is why the day comes with fireworks that light up the night sky, parades with flags and music, and picnics with family and friends. When your child colours the fireworks and flags on these pages, they are colouring the birthday party of a whole country.

About the Flag on This Page

The flag on this sheet is the flag of the United States, and every part of it means something. It has 50 stars — one for each of the 50 states — and 13 stripes for the first 13 colonies that became the country.

Its colours are red, white and blue. Count the stars together as your child colours them in — fifty is a big counting challenge, and the stripes are easier: just thirteen.
