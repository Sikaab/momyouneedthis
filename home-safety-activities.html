Home is where kids feel safest — and where most childhood bumps happen. These printable activities turn home safety into a detective game, helping young children spot hazards and learn the rules that keep everyone safe.

🏠 Learn About Home Safety

Children hunt for hazards in illustrated rooms — a spill on the floor, medicine within reach, an open gate — circle them, and sort everyday choices into “safe” and “ask a grown-up” groups. A simple family-rules checklist (we walk inside, we ask before opening the door) rounds out the sheet.

🔍 Do A Real Safety Walk

After the printable, walk through your home together at your child’s eye level and spot real hazards — cleaning supplies, unlocked cabinets, dangling cords. Let your child be the detective who finds them. Then fix what you can together and explain the rest simply.

💡 Why Kids Should Learn To Spot Hazards

Rules like “don’t touch” work best when kids understand the why. Spotting hazards in pictures — then in real rooms — builds the observation habit that keeps them safer even when you are not right beside them.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What home safety should preschoolers learn?

To notice common hazards (spills, sharp objects, medicines, open doors), follow simple family rules, and always ask a grown-up about anything unsafe or unfamiliar. These activities teach spotting and sorting for ages 2 to 5.

Are these printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

Should I childproof instead of teaching?

Both. Childproofing protects toddlers now; teaching builds the judgment they will need as they grow more independent. The printable’s detective game makes the learning part genuinely fun.
