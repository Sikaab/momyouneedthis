🗓️ When Should I Start Teaching These Time Words?

Most children start grasping yesterday, today, and tomorrow between ages 3 and 5. Start casually in conversation — “Yesterday we went to the park!” — and use the printable sorting game to make the words concrete and visual.

💡 Why Are Time Words So Tricky for Preschoolers?

Time is invisible, so words like yesterday and tomorrow feel abstract to young children. Picture sorting gives them something to see and touch, connecting each word to real events from their own life.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What do yesterday, today, and tomorrow mean for a preschooler?
Yesterday means something that already happened, today means right now, and tomorrow means something coming soon. Preschoolers learn these best through real examples — “Yesterday we saw grandma, today we are playing, tomorrow we go swimming.”

Are these time word printables really free?

How can we practice these words every day?
Make it a dinner-table ritual: ask your child to name one thing from yesterday, one thing from today, and one thing they hope for tomorrow. A few minutes of daily practice builds the concept faster than any worksheet alone.
