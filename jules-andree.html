WHY WE'RE HERE

Why We Think Moms Should Know Her

We found Jules. And immediately understood the chaos.

You know when someone describes motherhood in a way that is so specific you immediately think, "Oh. She gets it"?

That's Jules Andree.

Jules is a Colorado mom of four who has built her corner of the internet around honest, slightly unhinged motherhood — and honestly, that description does a lot of heavy lifting.

Her content captures the parts of family life that are sweet, ridiculous, exhausting and completely worth laughing about. One minute you're watching newborn and sibling chaos. The next, you're laughing at a parenting moment that feels suspiciously familiar.

And then there's the fact that she ran a marathon six months postpartum. Which feels like the sort of information that deserves its own moment of silence because... ma'am. 😂
