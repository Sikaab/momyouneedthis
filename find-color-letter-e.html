👀 Look Closely

Some letters look tricky and similar! Take it slow and check each letter. Saying “E says eh” each time you find one reinforces the sound too.

Make It Easier, Make It Harder

The same sheet works for a first hunt and a fifth one — adjust the challenge, not the page.

Easier: colour the big uppercase E's together first, so the shape is familiar before the hunting starts. A dot marker turns each find into a quick stamp.

Harder: lowercase e's only, a gentle timer against yesterday's try, or a race against a grown-up with their own copy — grown-ups lose surprisingly often.

🌈 Rainbow Challenge

For extra fun, color each E a different color of the rainbow. It turns a letter hunt into an art project.

E Words to Say While You Hunt

The game on this page is find, colour, SAY — and saying needs words. The three starters on the sheet (elephant, egg, eye) say E's short sound, "eh". These add more words to say together — and two honest surprises:

  • Elephant — eh-lephant. The biggest word on the sheet.
  • Egg — eh-gg. Small word, short sound.
  • Eye — E spells its whole sound here: "eye"!
  • Elbow — eh-lbow. Point at yours and bend it.
  • Envelope — eh-nvelope. The one letters travel in.
  • Elf — eh-lf. Short E, pointy ears.
  • Ear — E being sneaky: in "ear", E sounds like "here", not "eh".
  • Eagle — E stretches out long here: "ee-gle", the sound E makes in "me".

Six words say the short "eh" this page teaches; ear and eagle show that E has more than one trick — a good thing to know out loud, not a rule to memorise.
