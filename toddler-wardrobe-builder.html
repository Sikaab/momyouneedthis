    How many clothes does a toddler actually need?

    The answer depends much more on your family's routine than on your toddler's age. A family that does laundry every day can comfortably own fewer clothes than a family that washes once a week. Daycare, potty training and frequent outfit changes can also make a significant difference.

    Instead of giving every family the same giant toddler clothing checklist, this calculator adjusts the wardrobe around those variables.

    What should be in a toddler wardrobe?

    A practical toddler wardrobe usually includes everyday tops and bottoms, pajamas, underwear, socks, layers and a small number of special or dressier outfits.

    The goal isn't to own a certain number because a checklist says so. The goal is to have enough clean clothing to get through your normal laundry cycle while keeping a few backups for the inevitable toddler mess.

    Toddler clothes for daycare

    Daycare can change the equation because toddlers may need spare clothes for food spills, water, outdoor play, mud, diaper leaks and potty accidents.

    If your child attends daycare, keeping a small dedicated backup supply can prevent the need to constantly pull clothing from the main wardrobe.

    Toddler clothes for potty training

    Potty training often means temporarily increasing the number of underwear and easy-to-change bottoms you keep available. This doesn't necessarily mean buying an entirely new wardrobe.

    Once potty training becomes more predictable, you can reduce the backup supply again.

    FAQ

    How many outfits should a 2-year-old have?

    There isn't one universal number. A practical wardrobe depends on laundry frequency, outfit changes and whether your child attends daycare. Use the calculator above to create a starting number for your household.

    How many pajamas does a toddler need?

    A toddler who goes through pajamas quickly may need more pairs than a child who wears the same pajamas for several nights. Your laundry frequency is therefore one of the biggest factors.

    How many clothes should I keep at daycare?

    A few complete backup outfits can be useful, especially during messy stages, potty training and outdoor seasons. The exact number depends on your daycare's requirements and how frequently your child needs changes.
