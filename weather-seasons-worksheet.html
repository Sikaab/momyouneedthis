How To Use This Worksheet

Print the worksheet at home and let your child explore the different types of weather and seasons. Talk together about what the weather looks and feels like outside and which season you are currently in.

You can also use crayons, markers, or colored pencils to make the activity more interactive and encourage your child to talk about what they see.

Why Learn About Weather & Seasons?

Learning about weather and seasons helps young children connect everyday experiences with new vocabulary and concepts. It can also encourage observation, conversation, and early science learning.
