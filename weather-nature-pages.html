☁️ What Will We Learn About Weather?

Weather is what the sky is doing right now — sunny, cloudy, rainy, snowy, or windy. This printable introduces five kinds of weather with big friendly pictures, plus the clothes we wear for each: sunglasses for sun, boots for rain, mittens for snow. Children match weather pictures to the right outfits and scenes.

📋 How To Use These Activities

Print the weather sheet and start by looking out the window together: "What is the weather today?" Then work through the matching activities — draw lines from each weather picture to the right clothing or activity. Keep a week-long weather chart on the fridge where your child draws the daily weather.

🌦️ Be A Weather Reporter

Give your child a "reporter" job each morning: look outside and announce the weather like on TV. “Today is cloudy with a chance of puddles!” Dress a stuffed animal in the right weather clothes. On rainy days, watch drops race down the window — the best weather science needs no printable at all.

💡 Why Learn About Weather?

Weather is science children can observe every single day, which makes it the perfect first science topic. It builds vocabulary (drizzle, gust, frost), observation habits, and an understanding of cause and effect — "It rained, so the ground is wet." Plus, weather talk is a lifelong social skill!
