Turn learning into a game! Ask your child to find exactly ONE of something — one spoon, one shoe, one book. Hold up one finger together and say “one!” every time you find it.

💡 Why Start With the Number 1?

The number 1 is the foundation of all counting. When children understand that 1 means a single object, they build the one-to-one correspondence skill that makes all future math possible.
