🔎 Say The Words Slowly

Say each word slowly and emphasize the first sound. Ask your child, "Does it start with the A sound?"

Try familiar words such as apple, ant and alligator when practising the A beginning sound.

🧠 Make It A Listening Game

Look around the house or while you're out and about for objects that start with the A sound. Take turns finding an object and saying its name.

Keep the activity playful and focus on listening for the beginning sound rather than expecting perfect pronunciation.
