🔊 How Does It Work?

Look at each picture, say its name out loud and listen carefully to the first sound. Then color the picture while repeating the beginning sound together.

🍎 Start With Letter A

💡 Make Sound Practice Natural

You don't need to quiz your child while they color. Simply say the picture's name, emphasize the beginning sound naturally and invite your child to repeat it with you.
