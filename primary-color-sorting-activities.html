❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why start sorting with three colors?

Three groups are manageable for young minds — enough to require real decisions, but not so many that children feel overwhelmed.

Are these sorting printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

What if my child puts things in the wrong group?

Ask what color it is and let them compare it to the group label. Self-correction teaches more than being told the answer.
