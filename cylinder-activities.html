🥫 Learn About the Cylinder

Children learn that a cylinder has two flat circle ends and one curved side that rolls — just like a soup can, a toilet paper roll, or a birthday candle.

🥁 Cylinder Drum Parade

Turn learning into a game! Grab empty cans or paper rolls and tap them like drums. Feel the two flat circle ends and the curvy side — that rolling shape is a cylinder!

💡 Why Learn About the Cylinder?

Cylinders are everywhere in a child's world — food cans, crayon boxes, rolls, and cups. Naming this shape helps children see geometry in everyday objects and builds the vocabulary for describing 3D things.
