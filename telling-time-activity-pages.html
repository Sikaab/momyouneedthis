🧠 Why Learn To Tell Time?

Learning about time helps children begin understanding the sequence of daily events, routines, hours and the idea that time moves forward.

Simple clock activities give children an opportunity to connect what they see on a clock with familiar moments in their everyday lives.

🕐 Start With O'Clock Time

Begin with simple clocks showing whole hours. Help your child look at the hour hand and identify which number it is pointing toward.

What ages is this printable best for?
