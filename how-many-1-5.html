How To Use This Printable

Look at each group of objects with your child and ask how many they see. Encourage your child to count each object carefully and choose or identify the number that matches the group. You can also practice by counting toys, blocks, snacks, or other everyday objects around the house.

Why Practice How Many?

Learning to connect quantities with numbers helps children develop early number sense, one-to-one correspondence, counting, and number recognition. Practicing numbers from 1 to 5 gives young children a simple foundation for early math skills.
