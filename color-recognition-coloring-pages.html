🔍 Play “Bring Me Something”

After coloring, ask your child to bring you something blue from around the house. This real-world hunt transfers the color word from paper to life — and toddlers adore the mission.

💡 Why Color Words Matter

Color vocabulary is one of the earliest descriptive language skills, and it feeds directly into art, reading descriptions, and following instructions (“color the sky blue”). Focused practice with one color at a time prevents the common red-orange or blue-purple mix-ups.
