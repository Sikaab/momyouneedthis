🔷 Learn 2D vs 3D

Children learn that 2D shapes are flat like drawings (circle, square, triangle) while 3D shapes are solid like real objects (sphere, cube, cone) — and that every 3D shape has a flat 2D friend.

🤲 Flat or Solid Touch Test

Turn learning into a game! Hold up a paper circle (flat 2D) and a ball (solid 3D). Let your child touch both — can they feel the difference? Flat like paper versus solid like a ball!

💡 Why Learn 2D vs 3D?

Understanding flat versus solid is the gateway to all 3D geometry. Once children grasp that a drawing of a circle is flat but a ball is solid, they can start naming and sorting the 3D world around them.
