❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the primary colors?

Red, blue, and yellow. They are called primary because they cannot be made by mixing other colors — but they mix to make every other color.

Are these matching printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

Should we learn primary colors before others?

Yes, it is the ideal order. Once red, blue, and yellow are solid, secondary colors like green and purple make intuitive sense.
