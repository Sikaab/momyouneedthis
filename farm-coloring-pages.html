Farm Words to Say While You Color

Colouring is prime talking time. Every sheet on this page is full of farm words to name together:

  • Farm scene — point out the barn, the cow in the field, the tractor and the sunshine.
  • Pig — the pig is splashing in its mud puddle; pigs roll in mud to stay cool.
  • Horse — the horse stands by the fence with a hay bale; hay is the horse's food.
  • Chicken — the hen is sitting on her nest of eggs; chicks hatch from eggs.
  • The crop sheets — corn grows tall on stalks, pumpkins ripen on vines, sunflowers turn to the sun, wheat is tied into sheaves, and the vegetable garden grows carrots, cabbages and tomatoes.

Ask your child to be the farmer: who needs feeding first, and what sound does each animal make?
