How To Use These Coloring Pages

Talk about each feeling while your child colors. Ask questions like "When do you feel happy?" or "What makes you feel sad?" You can also use the pages during quiet time, preschool activities, or as part of an emotional learning lesson.

Why Learn About Feelings?

Learning to recognize and name emotions helps children build emotional vocabulary and communicate how they feel. Talking about feelings can also help children understand their own emotions and recognize feelings in others.
