Step by Step: How to Do This Activity

  1. Print the page. You need the printed page, child-safe scissors and a glue stick.
  2. Cut out the pictures. Snip each picture out along its edges — grown-ups can pre-cut for younger children.
  3. Name each colour together. As a picture comes free, say its colour out loud: 'This one is red!'
  4. Paste each picture on its matching colour circle. Match the picture's colour to the same colour circle and glue it down.
  5. Count your matches. Finish by counting how many pictures landed on each colour.

🔍 Safety Scissors Only

Use child-safe scissors and stay close by. Cutting practice is wonderful for hand strength — just keep it supervised and pressure-free.

💡 Why Cut And Paste?

Cutting builds the hand muscles needed for writing, while pasting decisions reinforce color matching. Two essential preschool skills in a single joyful craft.

Make It Easier, Make It Harder

Make it easier: pre-cut the pictures yourself and start with just two colours on the table. Your child does the naming, matching and pasting — the thinking parts — while scissor skills catch up.

Make it harder: after the page is finished, go on a colour hunt. Find one more object of each colour somewhere in the room and draw it on the back of the page, or name it and point.
