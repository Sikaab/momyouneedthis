🔤 Alphabet Activities for Preschoolers

These alphabet activities for preschoolers make learning letters feel like play. Tracing, coloring, and matching letters build recognition and early reading skills — all on free printable pages. Our alphabet activities for preschoolers are designed for ages 3 to 5.

The best alphabet activities for preschoolers mix tracing with games. Trace the letter A, then find all the A's on the page, then color them in. That trace-find-color loop cements the letter shape and sound together.

Tip: do alphabet activities for preschoolers one letter at a time — master A before moving to B, and always end with a letter your child already knows for a confidence boost.
