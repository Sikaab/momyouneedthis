🧠 Why Learn About The Five Senses?

Learning about the five senses helps children understand how they experience the world around them. It builds vocabulary, observation skills, curiosity, and an early understanding of the human body.

Five senses learning can happen through everyday experiences like listening to birds, touching different textures, smelling flowers, tasting foods, or noticing colors and shapes.

✋ Start With One Sense

Choose one sense and explore it together. Talk about what your child can see, hear, touch, taste, or smell and encourage them to describe what they notice.
