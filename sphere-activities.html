⚽ Learn About the Sphere

Children learn that a sphere is round all the way around with no flat sides and no corners — which is why balls roll so well! Think soccer balls, oranges, and bubbles.

🎾 Rolling Sphere Race

Turn learning into a game! Gather balls and other round objects and race them down a ramp or across the floor. Which ones roll the farthest? Those smooth rollers are spheres!

💡 Why Learn About the Sphere?

The sphere is the perfect first 3D shape because children already know it — every ball they have ever played with is a sphere. Connecting the shape name to something familiar makes 3D geometry click fast.
