❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What does match same colors mean?

Finding two things that are the same color and pairing them — the simplest form of color matching and a great first step.

Are these matching printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

What if my child matches by shape instead?

That is great thinking too. Gently redirect: this time we are matching by color. Try covering the shapes and focusing only on the colors.
