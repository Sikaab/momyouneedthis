🔎 Say The Words Together

Before coloring, say the name of each picture together. Encourage your child to listen to the first sound in each word.

Words such as apple, ant and alligator are familiar examples of words that begin with the A sound.

🖍️ Make Coloring Part Of The Lesson

Ask your child to color the pictures that begin with the A sound and leave the other pictures uncolored.

Afterward, talk about the pictures they colored and repeat the beginning sound together.
