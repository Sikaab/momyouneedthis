▭ All About the Rectangle

A rectangle has 4 sides and 4 corners, but unlike a square, its sides aren't all equal — two are long and two are short. Kids can find rectangles in doors, books, phones, and envelopes.

💡 Why Color Rectangles?

Learning rectangles after squares teaches children that shapes in the same family can look different. This comparison skill — noticing what's the same and what's different — is the heart of early math thinking.
