🔎 Can You Match Uppercase With Lowercase?

Look at two letters together and talk about how they belong to the same letter. For example, A and a look different, but they are both the letter A.

Look for uppercase and lowercase letters in books, signs, labels and familiar words. Ask your child if they can spot both forms.

🧠 Make The Connection

Point to A and say "uppercase A." Then point to a and say "lowercase a." Repeat that they are both the letter A.

Keep practising through everyday activities instead of turning it into a formal lesson. The more often children see the two forms together, the easier the connection can become.
