🔗 How Are A & a Connected?

Uppercase A and lowercase a look different, but they represent the same letter and the same sound. Seeing them together helps children begin to recognize that letters can have different forms.

💡 Try This With Your Child

Point to A and then a. Say, "These are both A!" Then let your child trace each one and look for A and a in books, signs and familiar words.

Why Practise Both Letter Forms?

Practising uppercase and lowercase letters together helps children build letter recognition while developing pencil control, hand coordination and early writing confidence.
