🔎 Can You Match A With a?

Look at the two letters together. Tell your child that A and a are different-looking forms of the same letter.

Then look for A and a in books, signs, labels and familiar words and see if your child can spot both forms.

🧠 Make The Connection

Point to A and say "uppercase A." Then point to a and say "lowercase a." Repeat that they are both the letter A.

Keep practising through everyday activities instead of turning it into a formal lesson. The more often children see the two forms together, the easier the connection can become.
