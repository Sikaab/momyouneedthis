🧠 Why Practise Shape Patterns?

Recognizing patterns helps children notice similarities, differences and sequences. Shape patterns also give children an opportunity to practise visual thinking, prediction and early problem-solving skills.

🎨 Start With A Simple Shape Pattern

Begin with an easy repeating pattern. Ask your child to look at the shapes, say the pattern out loud and decide which shape should come next.

💡 Let Your Child Explain Their Thinking

Instead of immediately telling your child which shape comes next, ask "What do you notice?" or "What do you think comes next?" Let them explain their choice and discover the pattern themselves.
