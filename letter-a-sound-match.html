🔤 How To Use This Printable

  1. Download and print the letter pages on letter-size paper.
  2. Say the letter name and sound together before your child starts.
  3. Trace, color, or match the letters, going slowly and having fun.
  4. Play I-spy with the letter around the house to lock in the learning.

🔎 Listen For The A Sound

Say familiar A words slowly and encourage your child to listen for the beginning sound.

Try words such as apple, ant and alligator and ask your child which ones start with the A sound.

🧠 Make It A Game

Look around your home for objects that begin with the A sound. Take turns finding something and saying its name together.

Keep the activity playful and focus on listening and recognizing sounds rather than expecting perfect pronunciation.
