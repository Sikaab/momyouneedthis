🍂 What Are The Four Seasons?

Spring brings flowers and baby animals, summer brings sunshine and swimming, fall brings colorful leaves and pumpkins, and winter brings snow and cozy mittens. This printable helps children connect each season with its weather, clothes, holidays, and activities — turning the passing year into something they can see and sort.

📋 How To Use These Activities

Print the seasons sheet and name each picture together first. Then ask your child to sort the pictures into the four season boxes — by drawing lines, cutting and pasting, or just pointing. Talk about which season it is right now and what you see outside your own window today.

🪟 Seasons Window Watch

Tape the four season cards to a window and keep a "seasons journal" — each week, have your child point to the current season and draw one thing they see outside. Over the year they will watch the world change: buds, then leaves, then colors, then bare branches. It is slow science at its best.

💡 Why Learn About The Seasons?

Seasons teach children that the world follows patterns — one of the biggest ideas in early science. Learning season words (blossom, harvest, frost) grows vocabulary, while sorting seasonal pictures builds classifying skills. It also helps kids understand time passing in a way calendars alone cannot.
