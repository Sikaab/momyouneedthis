🔎 How Does Letter Sound Matching Help?

When children match a letter with familiar pictures that begin with its sound, they begin connecting the written letter to spoken language. This is an important early step toward phonics and reading.

🍎 Try Letter A

Say the names of the pictures together and listen for the beginning sound. Then help your child decide which pictures belong with the letter A.

🎁 Start Letter A Activity

💡 Make It A Game

Say a picture's name slowly and emphasize the beginning sound. Ask, "Does this start with the A sound?" Let your child point, match or move the picture rather than turning the activity into a test.
