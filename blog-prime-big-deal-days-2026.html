📅 When Do the October Sales Happen?

Three sale events overlap this year, which is good news if you like comparing prices:

  • Amazon Prime Big Deal Days — October 6–7. 48 hours starting 12:01 a.m. PDT (3:01 a.m. EDT) on October 6. New deals drop at midnight, 8 a.m. and 1 p.m. PDT — that's 3 a.m., 11 a.m. and 4 p.m. EDT — so the mornings of both days are worth a check. Prime membership required.
  • Target Circle Deal Days — October 6–7. Same two days, with early access October 5 for paid Circle 360 members. Regular Circle membership is free, and their preview leads with 30% off baby home gear.
  • Walmart Fall Deals — October 5–11. A full week with no membership required. Worth a glance for anything Amazon and Target don't discount enough.

My honest take: for the categories below, Amazon's event usually wins on toys and brand-specific baby gear, while Target is worth checking for nursery and home items. Compare the final price in both carts before you buy — it takes two minutes.

🧠 How to Shop the Sale Without Regret

A few things I'd do before October 6:

  • Make your list now. Write down the 3–5 baby things you actually need before the sale starts. Deal pages are designed to make you buy things you didn't come for.
  • Set a target price for each item. Check what it costs today, then decide what discount would make it a yes. That way you're comparing against reality, not against an inflated "was" price.
  • Check both Amazon and Target. With both events on the same days, the same product can be cheaper at one or the other — especially nursery and home gear, where Target leads.
  • Don't wait for Black Friday on things you need now. For baby gear, October deal days often match November pricing on the same brands. If the crib soother is dying today, October is fine.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🏷️ Prime Big Deal Days Questions, Answered

Quick answers to the questions parents ask most about the October sale events.

When is Prime Big Deal Days 2026?

Amazon's Prime Big Deal Days runs Tuesday, October 6 through Wednesday, October 7, 2026 — 48 hours starting at 12:01 a.m. PDT (3:01 a.m. EDT) on October 6. New deals drop at midnight, 8 a.m., and 1 p.m. PDT each day, so checking back morning and afternoon is worth it. A Prime membership is required to shop the deals.

What baby products are usually discounted during Prime Big Deal Days?

The baby categories that typically see the deepest cuts are toys (often up to 60% off), strollers (up to $340 off in past events), car seats (up to $100 off), and brands like Momcozy, Maxi-Cosi, Graco, and Frida Baby (often 20–50% off). Breast pumps and baby monitors are also regularly flagged as top-value categories.

Do I need a Prime membership for Big Deal Days?

Yes. Prime Big Deal Days deals are exclusive to Prime members, unlike some other Amazon sale events. If you don't have Prime, the 30-day free trial covers the event — just remember to decide before it renews if you only wanted it for the deals.

Is Target Circle Deal Days the same days?

Yes — Target Circle Deal Days runs the same two days, October 6–7, 2026, with early access on October 5 for paid Circle 360 members. Regular Target Circle membership is free. Their preview leads with 30% off baby home gear, so it's worth comparing prices between the two events before buying.

Should I buy now or wait for Black Friday?

For baby and toddler products, October deal days often match Black Friday pricing on the same items — brands like Graco, Frida Baby, and toy makers discount heavily at both. If you need something soon (a coming birthday, a worn-out crib soother), buying during Big Deal Days is reasonable. If it's a nice-to-have, you can compare again in November.

How do I know a deal is actually good?

Check the price history before you buy — Amazon shows a "typical price" reference on many listings, and a quick search of the product name plus "price history" tells you whether the sale price is genuinely low or just looks low next to an inflated list price. Set a mental target price for anything on your list before the event starts.
