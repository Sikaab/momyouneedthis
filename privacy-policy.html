Privacy Policy MomYouNeedThis

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit MomYouNeedThis and how it may be used.

1. About This Privacy Policy

MomYouNeedThis ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website to provide practical information, resources, activities, product recommendations and helpful content for parents and families.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and shared when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

You may browse most of our website without directly providing personal information.

If you contact us, subscribe to a service, submit information through a form, or otherwise voluntarily provide information, we may receive information such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

We only use information for legitimate purposes related to operating, maintaining and improving the website and communicating with visitors.

Like many websites, MomYouNeedThis may automatically receive certain technical information when visitors access the website.

This may include information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, pages visited, referring pages, date and time of visits and general website usage information.

This information may be used to understand how visitors use the website, improve our content and maintain website performance.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

MomYouNeedThis may use cookies and similar technologies to help the website function, understand website traffic and improve the user experience.

If Google AdSense or other advertising services are used on this website, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to websites on the Internet.

Users may learn more about Google's advertising settings and available choices through Google's advertising settings.

5. Google AdSense and Advertising

If advertising is displayed on MomYouNeedThis, we may use Google AdSense or other advertising providers.

These providers may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to help deliver and measure advertisements.

Third-party advertising providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

We do not control the information collected directly by third-party advertising providers.

6. Google Analytics and Website Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors interact with our website.

Analytics information may include pages viewed, approximate geographic location, device information, browser information, traffic sources and general website activity.

Analytics information is used to improve the website and understand which content is useful to visitors.

Affiliate relationships do not increase the price you pay.

For more information, please see our Affiliate Disclosure .

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including retailers, social media platforms and other resources.

Once you leave MomYouNeedThis, the privacy practices of the third-party website apply. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

9. Children's Privacy

MomYouNeedThis is intended for parents, caregivers and adults. Our website is not directed toward children as an audience and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review and address the situation.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning personal information collected about you, including rights to access, correct, delete or restrict certain uses of your information.

You may also be able to control cookies through your browser settings and manage advertising preferences through available advertising platform settings.
