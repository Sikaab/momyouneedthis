🌀 What Are Spiral Lines?

Children learn that spiral lines curl round and round — like a snail shell, a cinnamon roll, or water going down the drain. Tracing them teaches the hand to keep a steady curve going.

💡 Why Practice Line Tracing?

❓ Line Tracing FAQs

What is spiral line tracing?

Spiral line tracing means following a line that starts at a green dot on the outer edge and curls round and round inward to the center, like a snail shell. It builds steady circular motion and control, the foundation for round letters.

How do I use these line tracing worksheets with my child?

Print the worksheet and let your child trace each line slowly with a crayon or marker. Start with finger tracing, celebrate smooth lines, and keep sessions short and playful — five happy minutes beats twenty frustrated ones.
