Baby Sleep Tracker Questions

What is a wake window?

A wake window is the amount of time your baby can comfortably stay awake between sleep periods.

Does every baby need the same schedule?

No. This tool provides a starting point based on age, but every baby has individual sleep needs.

Why track naps instead of following a strict schedule?

Babies often have different sleep needs each day. Tracking helps you adjust based on your baby's actual sleep.
