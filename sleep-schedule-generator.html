Baby Sleep Schedule Questions

What is a wake window?

A wake window is the amount of time a baby can comfortably stay awake between sleep periods. It changes as babies grow.

Should bedtime change if naps are short?

Yes. When daytime sleep is shorter than expected, an earlier bedtime can help prevent overtiredness.

Is every baby schedule the same?

No. This tool provides an evidence-informed starting point, but every baby has individual sleep needs.
