Baby Sleep Tracker Questions

What is a wake window and why does it matter?

A wake window is the amount of time your baby can comfortably stay awake between sleep periods. Tracking wake windows can help you recognize when your baby may be ready for their next nap or bedtime.

Can this sleep tracker create a schedule for my baby?

This tool provides a personalized starting point based on your baby's age and naps. Every baby is different, so use it as a guide and adjust based on your baby's sleep cues.

How many naps should my baby have each day?

Nap needs change as babies grow. Younger babies often need more frequent sleep periods, while older babies usually transition toward fewer, longer naps.

Why should I track naps instead of following a strict schedule?

Babies' sleep needs can change from day to day. Tracking naps helps you understand your baby's patterns and make small adjustments when needed.
