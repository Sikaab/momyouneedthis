🔍 Set Up Smelling Jars

Put a cotton ball dabbed with vanilla, lemon juice, or cinnamon in small jars with holes poked in the lids. Let your child sniff and guess each one, then match it to the pictures on the printable. Keep scents mild and food-based, and always supervise.

💡 Why Smell Activities Are Worth Doing

Smell is powerfully linked to memory and emotion — it is why a whiff of sunscreen can feel like summer. Exploring scents builds sensory vocabulary and body awareness, and it is often the sense young children enjoy most once they start paying attention to it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are sense of smell activities for preschoolers?

Sniffing and sorting games — guessing mild kitchen scents, sorting yummy vs. yucky smells, and matching scents to pictures. This printable includes safe, parent-led ideas designed for ages 2 to 5.

Are these printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

Are smelling games safe for toddlers?

Stick to mild, food-based scents (vanilla, lemon, cinnamon), keep everything supervised, and never let children sniff directly from bottles. The printable’s sorting activities need no real scents at all.
