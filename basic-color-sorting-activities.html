❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is color sorting?

Grouping objects by their color — all the red things together, all the blue things together. It teaches children to classify by what they observe.

Are these sorting printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

Sorting vs matching — what is the difference?

Matching pairs two same-colored things. Sorting groups many things into color categories — a bigger thinking step that builds on matching skills.
