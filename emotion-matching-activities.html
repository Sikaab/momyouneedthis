How To Use These Activities

Ask your child to look at each face and match it with the feeling it represents. Talk about times when they might feel happy, sad, angry, surprised, or worried. You can also cut out the pieces and turn the printable into a simple matching game.

Why Learn About Emotions?

Learning to recognize and name emotions helps children build emotional vocabulary and communicate their feelings. Matching activities also encourage observation, memory, and early social-emotional learning skills.
