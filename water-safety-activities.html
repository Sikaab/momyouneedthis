🏊 Learn About Water Safety

Children sort pool and bathtub scenarios into “safe” and “unsafe” — a grown-up within arm’s reach versus swimming alone, walking versus running on wet pool decks — and color a “water watcher” badge for the grown-up on duty. The golden rule repeats throughout: never go near water without a grown-up.

🔍 Assign A Water Watcher

At pools or beaches, one grown-up wears the “water watcher” role — phone down, eyes on the kids — and you rotate the job. The printable’s badge makes it official and fun, and it prevents the dangerous assumption that “someone” is watching.

💡 Why Repetition Saves Lives

Drowning is fast and silent, which is why water rules must be automatic, not just understood. Repeating the same simple rules — grown-up present, walk don’t run, never swim alone — through games and coloring builds the automatic habits that matter most.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What water safety rules should preschoolers know?

Never go near water without a grown-up, always swim with an adult present, walk (don’t run) near pools, and follow bathtub rules like no standing. These activities reinforce each rule through sorting and coloring.

Are these printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

Do water safety activities replace swim lessons?

No — they complement them. Swim lessons build skills; these activities build safety habits and awareness. Both matter, and neither replaces active grown-up supervision around water.
