☀️ Sunny Fun With cute summer coloring pages

Four sheets make up these cute summer coloring pages, each a single scene: an ice cream cone, a sandcastle, and a beach day.

The ice cream cone is the toddler pick: three stacked scoops, sprinkles, and a cherry on top mean three big color decisions — one flavor per scoop — with sun and clouds in the corners.

The sandcastle carries the most detail: three towers and a flag, a bucket and shovel, a starfish and a shell in the sand, waves behind. Older kids can shade each tower differently; younger ones can stop at the bucket and starfish.

Beach day sits between them: a beach ball and swim ring on a striped towel — one color per stripe for preschoolers — plus sunglasses, a palm tree, a smiling sun, and a starfish and shell. Print the cone first, then work up to the towel and the castle.
