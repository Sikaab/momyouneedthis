🎨 Which Pictures Earn Their Color? The E Rule

Elephant and egg earn their color on this page because they open with short e — the quiet “eh” that asks the ears to lean in. It is the subtlest sound in this set, which makes this page the connoisseur's round: no pop or blow announces the answer, just a soft vowel to catch.

E also owns its long sound — its name — and eye, one of this page's own words, carries it. Whether the eye gets colored sparks the best debate in the set. There is no wrong verdict if your child can say the word and defend the choice; that defence is more valuable than any single correct answer.

When the short-e pictures are colored, the gallery reads elephant, egg, elbow — the quiet team. Say them once more, softly, the way their sound deserves, and then let the leftovers be colored as loudly as your child wants.
