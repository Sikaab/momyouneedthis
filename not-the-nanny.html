WHY WE'RE HERE

Why We Think Moms Should Know Her

We found her. And immediately understood the assignment.

You know those accounts where you read one post, laugh way harder than you expected to and then immediately send it to another mom?

That's Not the Nanny.

Ana Balich has a way of taking the completely ordinary chaos of raising kids — the weird things they say, the parenting dilemmas, the marriage moments, the everyday ridiculousness — and turning it into something genuinely funny.

And that's what we love about her. It's not motherhood polished into something Instagram-perfect. It's motherhood with a sense of humor.

THE MOMYOUNEEDTHIS TEST

So... Why Her?

Because some mom accounts give you tips. Some give you inspiration. And some make you laugh when you desperately needed to laugh.

😂

She makes motherhood funny.

Not fake-perfect funny. Real-life funny. The kind where you recognize your own kids, your own house or your own questionable parenting decisions in the joke.

💗

There's heart underneath it.

The humor works because there's something genuinely relatable underneath it. Ana's content can make you laugh and still leave you feeling understood.

🌷

She feels like a mom friend.

And honestly, that's the test. The kind of account where you don't just laugh at the post — you want to send it to your friend and say, "THIS. This is exactly it."
