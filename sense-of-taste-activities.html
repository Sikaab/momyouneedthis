🔍 Play The Mystery Bite Game

With your child’s eyes closed (or just looking away), offer tiny tastes of familiar safe foods — banana, cheese, lemon — and let them guess. Talk about whether each taste is sweet, salty, or sour. Keep portions tiny and always check for allergies first.

💡 Why Exploring Taste Matters

Noticing and naming flavors builds vocabulary and sensory awareness, and playful tasting games can make cautious eaters more willing to try new foods. When tasting is framed as discovery rather than pressure, kids relax — and sometimes surprise you.
