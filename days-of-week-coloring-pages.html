❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do kids learn the days of the week?

Through repetition in routine — singing the sequence, marking “today” on a calendar, and connecting days to events (“park day is Saturday”). These coloring pages add a hands-on, visual layer for ages 2 to 5.

Are these coloring pages really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

My child mixes up the order. Is that normal?

Completely. The sequence takes many repetitions to stick. Keep the calendar page visible, sing the song often, and celebrate progress — most kids nail the order sometime during the preschool years.
