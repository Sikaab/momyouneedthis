🔍 Two-Rule Sorting At Home

Sort your crayons by color AND type! Or sort blocks by color and shape. Two rules make everything more interesting.

💡 Why Sort By Two Attributes?

Holding two rules in mind at once builds working memory and cognitive flexibility — top-tier thinking skills. It's challenging, so start with obvious differences (red circles vs. blue squares) before making it trickier.
