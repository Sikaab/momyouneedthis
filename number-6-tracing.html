6️⃣ Learn About The Number 6

Children learn that the number 6 is one more than five — an insect has six legs, and half a dozen eggs is six. The number 6 starts with a curve that loops into a circle, wonderful pencil practice.

🔍 Number 6 Hunt Around Your Home

Turn learning into a game! Count six of something — six grapes, six stickers, six toy animals. Arrange them in two rows of three and count each row, then all together!

💡 Why Learn The Number 6?

The number 6 introduces numbers beyond a single hand, stretching children's counting past five. Its looping shape builds the smooth curves kids will need for letters like G and C.
