🔁 How Do I Explain “Before” and “After” Simply?

Anchor the words to your child’s day: “We brush teeth BEFORE bed” and “We play AFTER lunch.” Once the words click in real life, the printable matching activities feel easy and fun.

💡 Why Teach Sequencing Before Clocks?

Telling time is really about order — what comes first and what comes next. Children who can sequence stories and daily events learn clock reading much faster because the underlying logic is already in place.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are time concepts for preschoolers?
Time concepts are the basic words and ideas behind time: before and after, first next and last, morning and night, yesterday and tomorrow. They are the foundation children need before clock reading makes sense.

Are these time concept printables really free?
Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

How do sequencing strips help with telling time later?
Sequencing strips teach children that events happen in a fixed order — the same logic clocks use. A child who can confidently order first, next, and last already understands the idea that time moves forward, which makes hour and minute hands far less confusing.
