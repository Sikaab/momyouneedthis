➡️ What Is Direction Practice?

Children follow arrows that show where to start and which way to move — down, across, or around. Learning correct direction early prevents bad habits, like writing letters bottom-to-top, that are hard to fix later.

💡 Why Practice Pre-Writing?

Correct stroke direction is one of the strongest predictors of fluent handwriting. Children who learn top-to-bottom, left-to-right habits early write faster and more legibly when school demands it.

❓ Pre-Writing FAQs

What is direction practice in pre-writing?

Direction practice teaches children to follow arrows showing where strokes start and which way they move. It builds correct top-to-bottom, left-to-right habits that make future handwriting faster and neater.
