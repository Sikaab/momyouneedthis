🧠 Why Learn About Animals?

Learning about animals encourages children to observe, ask questions and notice how living things are connected to the world around them. Animal topics are also a fun way to introduce early science concepts through things children already find fascinating.

🐾 Start With Animal Habitats

Begin by learning where animals live. Talk about what you notice, compare different habitats and ask your child why an animal might be well suited to the place where it lives.

🎁 Explore Animal Habitats

💡 Let Your Child Be Curious

You don't need to know every answer. Ask questions like "Where do you think this animal lives?" or "What do you notice about it?" Encourage your child to observe, make predictions and discover new things with you.
