Emergencies are rare, but knowing what to do brings confidence. These printable activities teach young children the essentials in a calm, age-appropriate way: find a grown-up, know the helpers, and practice the basics.

📞 Learn About Emergency Safety

Children identify community helpers (firefighters, police officers, paramedics, doctors), sort scenarios into “get a grown-up” versus “handle it myself” (a scraped knee vs. a stranger at the door), and — for older preschoolers with a grown-up — practice saying the emergency number 911. The emphasis stays on staying calm and finding help.

🔍 Teach “Find A Grown-Up First”

For toddlers and young preschoolers, the single most important rule is simple: if something feels wrong or scary, find a grown-up right away. Practice naming trusted grown-ups — at home, at preschool, at the park — so your child always has an answer.

💡 Why Calm Practice Beats Scary Warnings

Fear shuts down thinking; familiarity keeps it working. Brief, matter-of-fact practice — naming helpers, rehearsing “find a grown-up” — builds the calm recall kids need. Keep sessions short, upbeat, and focused on helpers, not hazards.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Should preschoolers learn 911?

Older preschoolers can begin learning it with a grown-up’s guidance, alongside the more important rule: find a trusted grown-up first. This printable introduces the number gently and keeps the focus on getting help, not on memorizing digits under pressure.

Are these printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

How do I keep this topic from being scary?

Talk about helpers, not dangers. Keep it short, use the printable’s friendly illustrations, and end with something reassuring — like naming all the grown-ups who keep them safe every day.
