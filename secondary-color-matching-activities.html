❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are the secondary colors?

Orange, green, and purple — each made by mixing two primary colors together.

Are these matching printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

When should we try secondary colors?

After the primary colors feel easy. If red, blue, and yellow matching is quick and confident, it is time for orange, green, and purple.
