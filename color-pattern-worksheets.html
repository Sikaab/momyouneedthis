🧠 Why Practise Patterns?

Recognizing patterns helps children learn to notice similarities, differences and sequences. These early skills support mathematical thinking, problem-solving and predicting what comes next.

🎨 Start With A Simple Pattern

Begin with an easy repeating pattern. Ask your child to look at the colors, say the pattern out loud and decide which color should come next.

💡 Let Your Child Explain Their Thinking

Instead of simply telling your child which color comes next, ask, "What do you notice?" or "What do you think comes next?" Letting them explain their choice makes pattern practice more meaningful.
