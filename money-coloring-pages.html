🔍 Play Store At Home

After coloring, set up a pretend store with toy price tags in cents and let your child “pay” with real coins. Handling actual money while remembering the colored pages cements names and values fast — and it is a blast.

💡 Why Money Sense Starts Early

Recognizing coins and understanding value lays groundwork for counting, addition, and real-world decision making. Early, playful exposure means money math feels familiar — not intimidating — when school introduces it.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What money concepts do these pages teach?

Coin recognition (penny, nickel, dime, quarter), each coin’s name and cent value, an introduction to dollar bills, and very simple value matching. It is a first-look foundation for ages 4 to 6, taught through coloring.

Are these coloring pages really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

Are the coin sizes accurate?

The illustrations are enlarged for coloring and little hands — great for learning names and values, not for size comparison. Use real coins alongside the pages when size matters.
