🧠 Why Practise "How Many?"

Counting pictures helps children connect quantities with numbers and build one-to-one counting skills. Asking "How many?" also encourages children to understand that the final number they count represents the total amount.

🍎 Start With A Simple Counting Activity

Ask your child to touch or point to each picture while counting. When they finish, ask, "How many are there?" and let them choose the number that matches the group.

💡 Encourage Careful Counting

If your child loses track, that's okay. Encourage them to start again and touch each picture once as they count. Keeping the activity relaxed helps build confidence with numbers.
