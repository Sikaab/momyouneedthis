🥁 Pattern Clap Game

Turn learning into a game! Clap a pattern together — clap-clap-stomp, clap-clap-stomp — then draw it on paper. Patterns are everywhere: in music, in nature, and in writing!

💡 Why Practice Patterns?

Patterns teach children to predict, repeat, and stay consistent — the same rhythm writing requires. Pattern practice also builds early math thinking, since recognizing sequences is the heart of both math and reading.
