🔎 What Is A Beginning Sound?

The beginning sound is the first sound we hear when we say a word. Learning to hear that sound helps children connect spoken words with the letters that represent them.

🍎 Start With Letter A

Say each word aloud with your child. Listen carefully to the first sound, then choose the letter that starts the word.

💡 Say The Word Out Loud

Hearing the word makes the activity easier. Say it slowly, emphasize the first sound naturally and let your child choose the matching letter.
