🫘 Grow A Bean Together

The classic windowsill bean experiment never fails: place a bean on damp cotton in a clear cup and watch roots appear in days. Have your child draw the bean’s progress each morning. Pair it with a nature walk to collect leaves — sort them by size, color, and shape back home.

💡 Why Learn About Plants?

Plants are the friendliest introduction to life science — they grow visibly, they do not run away, and caring for them teaches responsibility. Plant learning builds vocabulary (stem, petal, root), observation skills, and patience, all while getting little hands happily dirty.
