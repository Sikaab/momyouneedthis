❓ Christmas Bundle FAQs

How many pages are in the bundle?

Twelve. The bundle holds twelve Christmas printables: colouring pages, tracing, counting, patterns, a Santa maze, matching, colour-by-number, sorting, a wishlist page and a build-a-snowman page.

Can I print just one activity?

Yes. Every activity in the bundle has its own PDF, so you can download and print a single page without printing the other eleven.

What ages is the bundle for?

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers. The activities use big shapes, simple counting from 1 to 10 and gentle patterns, and grown-ups can help younger toddlers with the cutting and maze pages.

Is the Christmas bundle really free?

Yes — all twelve printables are 100% free to download and print at home. No signup and no payment, just tap the download buttons.
