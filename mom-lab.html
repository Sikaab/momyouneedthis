THE TESTING DEPARTMENT

Welcome to the unreasonable part.

These are the recurring tests products may face inside the Mom Lab. Because apparently regular product reviews weren't chaotic enough.

PROTOCOL 001
🧸
TODDLER RESISTANCE

Toddler Test

Can the product survive the tiny human who has decided that everything must be thrown, pulled, opened or completely ignored?

PRIMARY QUESTION Would a toddler destroy this in under five minutes?
TEST STATUS: BRUTAL
PROTOCOL 002
💥
STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY

Drop Test

Because things fall. Constantly. Especially the expensive things.

PRIMARY QUESTION Does it survive the inevitable drop?
TEST STATUS: GRAVITY APPROVED
PROTOCOL 003
🙃
REAL-LIFE CHAOS

Upside-Down Test

We turn it upside down because that's apparently how mom life eventually uses everything.

PRIMARY QUESTION Does everything stay where it belongs?
TEST STATUS: ZERO MERCY
PROTOCOL 004
👜
PORTABILITY

Diaper-Bag Test

If it cannot survive being buried under snacks, wipes, toys and mystery objects, we have questions.

PRIMARY QUESTION Can mom actually find and use it?
TEST STATUS: BAG COMPATIBILITY
PROTOCOL 005
🌙
SLEEP DEPRIVATION

3AM Test

Can a half-asleep mom figure it out without reading seventeen pages of instructions?

PRIMARY QUESTION Could mom use this at 3:07 AM?
TEST STATUS: SLEEP-DEPRIVED APPROVED
PROTOCOL 006
🧠
COGNITIVE LOAD

Mom Brain Test

We test whether the product still makes sense when mom has approximately three functioning brain cells left.

PRIMARY QUESTION Is this actually easy to use?
TEST STATUS: BRAIN CELL DEPENDENT
PROTOCOL 007
🚗
MOBILE MOM LIFE

Car Test

Cup holders, car seats, crumbs, one hand and absolutely no available surface area.

PRIMARY QUESTION Does it work in the car?
TEST STATUS: ROAD TESTED
PROTOCOL 008
🚫
TODDLER BEHAVIOUR

“Don't Touch That” Test

We tell the toddler not to touch it. You already know what happens next.

PRIMARY QUESTION Does it survive the forbidden-object effect?
TEST STATUS: HIGH RISK
PROTOCOL 009
🏆
FINAL BOSS

Mom Lab Survival Test

The product faces the full combination: toddler chaos, one hand, no sleep, diaper bag, gravity and absolutely no patience.

PRIMARY QUESTION Is this actually worth recommending to another mom?
FINAL STATUS: SURVIVAL OR FAILURE
