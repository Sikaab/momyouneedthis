Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mom Lab?

Mom Lab is MomYouNeedThis's product-research corner, where we dig through thousands of parent reviews and ratings to find the baby and toddler products that actually hold up in real mom life — and call out the ones worth skipping.

How does Mom Lab pick products?

We look at what real parents report: star ratings across thousands of reviews, value for the price, durability complaints, and how easy a product is to use one-handed at 3AM. Products that earn their place get the Mom Lab Pick badge; duds get an honest Skip It.

What happens to the products that make the cut?

Products that earn a Mom Lab Pick can face off in Mom Battles, where moms vote for their favorites.

Can I recommend a product for the Lab to research?

Yes. If there's a product you recommend to every mom who asks, share it through the Recommend a Product page.

Does MomYouNeedThis earn from the products it features?

Yes — product links on this page are Amazon affiliate links. If you buy through them, MomYouNeedThis may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.
