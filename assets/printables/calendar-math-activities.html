🧮 Calendar math! Read each question, think hard, and write your answer on the line!
1️⃣How many days are in a week?7️⃣
Answer:
There are 7 days: count them on your fingers!
2️⃣How many months are in a year?1️⃣2️⃣
Answer:
January to December — that's 12!
3️⃣How many seasons are in a year?4️⃣
Answer:
Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter!
4️⃣If today is Monday, what day is tomorrow?📅
Answer:
Tuesday comes after Monday!
5️⃣If your birthday is in June, how many months until December?6️⃣
Answer:
July, August, September, October, November, December!
