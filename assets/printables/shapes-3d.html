🧊 Shape Learning
3D Shapes
Shapes you can hold in your hand!
🖍️ These shapes are 3D — that means they pop out, like real toys! Point to each one and say its name.

Cube 🧊

🎲 Like a dice or a building block! 6 flat square faces • 8 corners

Sphere ⚽

⚽ Like a ball or an orange! Perfectly round all over • rolls everywhere!

Cone 🎉

🎉 Like a party hat or ice cream cone! Pointy on top • round on the bottom

Cylinder 🥫

🥫 Like a soup can or toilet paper roll! 2 circle faces • rolls on its side

Pyramid 🔺

🏜️ Like the pyramids in Egypt! Triangle sides • 1 pointy top
🌟 Shape hunt! Walk around your home and find one thing shaped like each 3D shape!
