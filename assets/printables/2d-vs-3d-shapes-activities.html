🔷 2D vs 3D Shapes
Flat or Solid?
Match every pair!
🔷 Draw a line matching each flat (2D) shape to its solid (3D) partner! 2D is flat like paper — 3D is solid like a real object you can hold.
👩‍👧 Parent tip: Hold a real ball next to a drawn circle while doing this. Feeling “flat like paper” versus “solid like a ball” makes the 2D/3D difference click instantly.
