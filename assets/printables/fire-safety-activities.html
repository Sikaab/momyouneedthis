🔥 Safety First
Stop, Drop & Roll!
Learn the 3 steps that keep you safe from fire!
✂️ Cut out the 3 steps and put them in order: what comes 1st, 2nd, 3rd? Then practice!
1
🛑
STOP
Stop where you are — don't run!
✂️ cut & order me!
2
🧎
DROP
Drop to the ground and cover your face!
✂️ cut & order me!
3
🌀
ROLL
Roll back and forth until the flames are out!
✂️ cut & order me!
🚨 If you see fire: get OUT, stay OUT, and tell a grown-up to call 911!
