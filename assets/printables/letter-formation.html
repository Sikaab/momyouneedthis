Letter Formation Practice
Follow the numbered strokes, then trace the big dashed letters!
🔢 Start at stroke ① and follow the numbers in order. Trace slowly — good writers take their time!

Aa — says "ah" like apple 🍎

1 Big A: down, down, across
1 Little a: around, then down

Bb — says "buh" like bee 🐝

1 Big B: straight down first
2 then the two bumps
1 Little b: down, then around

Cc — says "kuh" like cat 🐱

1 C is easy — just one curve!
Start at the top, swoop around.
