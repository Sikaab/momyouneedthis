⏱️ No-Prep Fun
5-Minute Toddler Activities
Boredom-buster cards — cut them out & pick one anytime!
✂️ Cut along the card edges, shuffle the deck, and let your toddler pick one! Each activity takes 5 minutes or less.
⏱️ 5 min
🎨
Sticker Sort
Give your toddler a sheet of dot stickers and let them stick them on paper — sort by color if they're ready!
Need: dot stickers + paper
⏱️ 5 min
💧
Water Painting
Hand them a cup of water and a paintbrush. "Paint" the fence, sidewalk, or construction paper and watch it disappear!
Need: water + paintbrush
⏱️ 3 min
🧦
Sock Toss
Roll up pairs of socks into balls. Toss them into a laundry basket — move it further back each round!
Need: rolled socks + basket
⏱️ 5 min
🔍
Color Hunt
Pick a color and race around the house finding 5 things that match. "Find something RED!"
Need: just your eyes!
⏱️ 4 min
🎵
Freeze Dance
Play music and dance! When the music stops, FREEZE like a statue. Silly poses encouraged.
Need: music
⏱️ 5 min
📦
Box Surprise
Put 5 household objects in a box. Let them reach in (no peeking!) and guess each one by touch.
Need: box + safe objects
