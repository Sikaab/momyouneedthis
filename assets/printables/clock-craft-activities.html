✂️ Clock Craft
Build Your Own Clock!
Craft a working clock and practice!
✂️ Build a clock! Make your own working clock, then practice setting the times below!
1️⃣
🍽️
Trace a paper plate (or draw a big circle) — this is your clock face!
2️⃣
🔢
Write the numbers 1–12 around the edge, just like a real clock!
3️⃣
✂️
Cut two hands from cardboard: one SHORT (hour) and one LONG (minute)!
4️⃣
📌
Pin the hands in the middle with a brad so they can spin!
5️⃣
🎮
Play! Grown-up calls a time, you move the hands to match!
🎯 Practice with YOUR clock: set the hands to match each time!
