My First Strokes
The 9 building blocks of handwriting — master them all!
🖍️ Trace each stroke, following the pink arrow direction. These strokes build every letter!
1. Tall Line ⬇️
top to bottom
2. Long Line ➡️
left to right
3. Circle ⭕
round and round
4. Cross ➕
down, then across
5. Square 🟧
four straight sides
6. Slant Line ↗️
diagonal up
7. Big X ❌
two slants cross
8. Triangle 🔺
three pointy sides
9. Curvy Hill 🌈
up and over
💡 Mom tip: Mastering these 9 strokes means your child can form every letter of the alphabet! Practice one row per day.
