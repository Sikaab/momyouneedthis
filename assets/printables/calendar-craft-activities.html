Make Your Own Calendar
Build a 12-month flip calendar!
✂️ Let's build a calendar! Follow the steps to make your very own 12-month flip calendar!
1️⃣
✂️
Cut out 12 small rectangles from paper — one for each month!
2️⃣
🖍️
Write one month name on each card and draw a tiny picture for it!
3️⃣
📌
Stack the cards in order and staple or clip the top!
4️⃣
🎨
Decorate the cover with your name and hang it on the fridge!
