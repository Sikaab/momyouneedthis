🛡️ Safety First
Safe or Not?
Talk through each story — then mark it safe or not!
💬 Read each story with a grown-up. Color the circle ✅ green if SAFE, ❌ red if NOT safe!
🧑‍🏫 A teacher you know says hello at school. ✅ color me GREEN!
👴 A stranger offers you candy at the park. ❌ color me RED!
👮 A police officer helps you find mom. ✅ color me GREEN!
🚶 Someone you don't know asks you to come with them. ❌ color me RED!
💡 Safety rule: if you feel unsafe, yell "NO!", run to a trusted grown-up!
