🩻 The Swimsuit Rule
Areas covered by a swimsuit are private. No one should touch them except to keep you healthy & clean.
🗣️ I Can Say NO!
You can always say NO to any touch you don't like. Then tell a trusted grown-up right away.
💡 Grown-up tip: teach the real names for body parts — it keeps kids safer.
