How does dinosaur color by number work?

Each dinosaur picture is split into numbered parts. Kids look at the color key, find the color for each number, and fill in the sections — the dinosaur appears as they color.

Can toddlers do color by number?

Yes, with a little help. Sit with your toddler, point to a number, name the matching color together, and let them color that section. It becomes a lovely shared activity.
