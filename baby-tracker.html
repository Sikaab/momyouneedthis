HOW IT WORKS

Free baby tracker in three taps

This free baby tracker is made for the 3am feed: log everything without typing a single word. No app to download, no account to create — it runs right in your phone's browser.

Why Track Feeds, Diapers and Sleep?

In the first weeks, every day can feel the same at 3am — but underneath, the pattern is changing fast. A simple log turns "I think she ate an hour ago" into real answers: when the last feed was, how many wet diapers there have been today, and how long that nap actually lasted.

The patterns are genuinely useful. A sudden jump in feeding often marks a growth spurt. A dip in wet diapers is worth noticing early. And when the pediatrician asks how feeding and sleeping are going, you have numbers instead of guesses — this tracker's daily summary counts and times everything for you.

Sleep adds up the same way. A few days of logged naps and night stretches show a rhythm forming — usually before it feels like one from inside the day.

The First Week Home: What to Log

The first week is the one week when the counts matter most. Log every feed, every diaper and every sleep stretch, and you will have exactly the numbers your baby's first checkups ask about.

  • Feeds — most newborns feed 8 to 12 times in 24 hours, day and night. Log each feed's time (and the side, if you are nursing) so the gaps are easy to see.
  • Wet diapers — expect roughly one wet diaper on day one, building through the week to at least six a day by the end of it. A dry stretch is easier to spot in a log than in memory.
  • Dirty diapers — the first stools are dark meconium; over the first days they turn green, then yellow. That colour change is a good sign feeding is working.
  • Sleep — newborns sleep in short stretches that add up to most of the day. Log the stretches, not just the nights, and the total takes care of itself.

After the first week, most families relax into logging feeds and sleep and spot-checking diapers — the tracker keeps the summary either way.

Is this baby tracker really free?

Yes. The MomYouNeedThis baby tracker is completely free — no account, no signup, no subscription. Open the page and start logging.

Do I need to download an app?

No. It runs right in your phone's browser — nothing to install, no app store, no updates. At 3am with a newborn, the fastest tracker is the one that's already open.

Where is my baby's data stored?

On your own device. Your logs are saved in your browser's local IndexedDB storage. Nothing is sent to our servers, and there is no cloud account holding your baby's information.

How do I log a feed with my voice?

Tap the microphone button and say what happened — for example, “Baby nursed on the left for 15 minutes” or “Baby drank 120 milliliters.” The tracker transcribes it and logs it. Prefer tapping? The quick-tap buttons log feeds, diapers, sleep and notes in one tap.

Can I log with my hands full?

Yes — that's exactly what voice logging is for. While nursing or holding your baby, just tap the microphone and say what happened. No typing, no forms, no fumbling with your phone one-handed.

Can I track naps and nighttime sleep?

Yes. Start the sleep timer when your baby falls asleep and end it when they wake. Naps are added to your daily summary automatically.

Can I back up my tracking data?

Yes. Use Export Backup to download your data as a file, and Import Backup to restore it on any device. You can delete everything at any time from settings.
