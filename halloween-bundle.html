🎲 Make the games last: print the Brooms & Webs board and memory cards on cardstock — or glue them onto cardboard — so they survive little hands. Laminate them at home and they'll last for years (kids can even use dry-erase markers on laminated cards and wipe them clean!).
✨ How to laminate with self-adhesive sheets:
  1. Print the game pages (cardstock works best). Cut out the memory cards.
  2. Peel the backing off one laminating sheet and lay it sticky-side up.
  3. Place your page or cards face-down onto the sticky side, smoothing from the center out to push air bubbles away.
  4. Trim the excess, leaving a small sealed border around the edges — done! No machine needed.
