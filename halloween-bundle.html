🎲 Make the games last: print the Brooms & Webs board and memory cards on cardstock — or glue them onto cardboard — so they survive little hands. Laminate them at home and they'll last for years (kids can even use dry-erase markers on laminated cards and wipe them clean!).
✨ How to laminate with self-adhesive sheets:
  1. Print the game pages (cardstock works best). Cut out the memory cards.
  2. Peel the backing off one laminating sheet and lay it sticky-side up.
  3. Place your page or cards face-down onto the sticky side, smoothing from the center out to push air bubbles away.
  4. Trim the excess, leaving a small sealed border around the edges — done! No machine needed.

❓ Halloween Bundle FAQs

Is the Halloween bundle really free?

Yes — all fifteen printables are free for personal and classroom use. No signup, no email, no catch: download the PDFs and print what you need.

What ages is the Halloween bundle for?

Babies to preschoolers. The contrast cards are made for babies; the pumpkin, ghost and witch colouring pages suit toddlers; and the tracing, counting, matching, maze, pattern and game pages are aimed at preschoolers.

What's inside the Halloween bundle?

Fifteen printables: pumpkin, ghost and witch colouring pages; pumpkin tracing; ghost counting 1 to 10; a candy colour sort; a pumpkin-faces matching page; a bat maze; a costume pattern page; a gummy-worm pattern page; bat colour by number; a trick-or-treat picture-to-word match; the Brooms & Webs board game; a 24-card memory game; and six Halloween contrast cards for babies.

How do I print the games so they last?

Print the memory game and the board game on cardstock, or glue the printed pages to cardboard before cutting — the Supplies section on this page lists exactly that trick, plus self-adhesive laminating sheets if you want them to survive several Octobers.

Can I print just one page instead of all fifteen?

Yes. Every printable in the bundle has its own Download PDF button, so you can print a single colouring page today and come back for the games later.
