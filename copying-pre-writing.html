👀 What Is Copying Practice?

Children study an example — a line, shape, or simple pattern — and then reproduce it in the empty space beside it. Copying is the bridge between tracing (following) and independent writing (creating from memory).

🪞 Mirror Game

Draw a simple shape and challenge your child to copy it exactly, like a mirror. Take turns being the leader — copying each other's drawings is giggle-filled practice!

💡 Why Practice Pre-Writing?

Copying develops visual-motor integration: the eyes see, the brain plans, the hand executes. This teamwork is exactly what writing requires, and copying practice strengthens it before letter shapes enter the picture.

❓ Pre-Writing FAQs

What is copying practice in pre-writing?

Copying practice shows children an example line, shape, or pattern and asks them to draw it themselves nearby. It builds the eye-hand teamwork of visual-motor integration that independent writing requires.

How do I use these pre-writing worksheets with my child?
