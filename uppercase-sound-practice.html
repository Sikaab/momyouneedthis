👂 Why Practise Letter Sounds?

Learning to connect a written letter with the sound it represents helps children develop early phonics skills and prepares them for connecting sounds to words when they begin reading.

🍎 Start With Letter A

Look at the uppercase A, say its sound together and practise finding words that begin with the same sound.

🎁 Start Letter A Sound Practice

💡 Keep It Playful

You can practise letter sounds while looking at books, playing with toys or noticing letters on signs. Say the letter and its sound naturally instead of turning every practice moment into a quiz.
