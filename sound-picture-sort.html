🔎 What Is A Beginning Sound?

The beginning sound is the first sound we hear when we say a word. Picture sorting gives children a chance to listen carefully and connect that sound with a written letter.

🍎 Try Letter A

Say each picture's name out loud together. Listen to the first sound, then decide whether the picture belongs in the Letter A group.

💡 Let Your Child Listen First

Instead of immediately telling your child the answer, say the picture's name slowly and ask, "What sound do you hear first?" Give them time to listen and make their own choice.
