🧠 Why Learn About Plants?

Learning about plants encourages children to observe nature, ask questions and understand how living things grow and change. Plants also provide an easy way to introduce early science concepts through hands-on experiences.

🌱 Start With How Plants Grow

Begin with a seed and follow its journey as it grows roots, a stem and leaves. Encourage your child to notice how plants change over time.

🎁 Explore How Plants Grow

💡 Let Your Child Explore Nature

Look for flowers, leaves, trees and plants together outside. Let your child touch safe plants, notice their shapes and colours, collect fallen leaves or help water a garden. Everyday experiences can become simple science learning moments.
