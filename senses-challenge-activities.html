🧠 What Is the Senses Challenge?

Solve sensory mysteries, sort objects by touch, sound and sight, and complete five-sense challenges. Where a first senses activity asks your child to name what they see or hear, the challenge pages ask them to combine senses — feeling an object without peeking, matching sounds, and figuring out which sense gives the answer.

It is a gentle step up for preschoolers who already know their five senses and are ready to put them to work together.

💡 How To Use This Activity

Print the activity below and explore it together in short, playful sessions of five to ten minutes. There is no right or wrong way to play — follow your child's curiosity, name what you see together, and celebrate every little discovery. Keep crayons handy and let the learning feel like playtime.
