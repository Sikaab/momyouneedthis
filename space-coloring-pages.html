Space Words to Know

The sheets on this page are full of space things. Learn the words that go with them:

  • Earth — our planet, the one we all live on.
  • Mars — the red planet, famous for its rusty colour.
  • Orbit — the path a planet or moon travels around and around.
  • Rocket launch — the blast-off moment when a rocket leaves the ground, like the rocket sheet on this page.
  • Telescope — a tool that makes faraway stars and planets look close.
  • Astronaut — a person who travels into space, like the waving astronaut on this page.
  • Galaxy — a huge family of stars; the planets sheet shows galaxies swirling around the planets.
