🧠 Why Practise Completing Patterns?

Completing patterns helps children notice order, repetition and relationships between objects. These early skills support prediction, logical thinking, problem-solving and early math development.

🎨 Start With A Simple Pattern

Begin with an easy repeating sequence. Ask your child to look at what comes before the missing space and think about what should come next.

🎁 Start AB Pattern Activity

💡 Ask "What Comes Next?"

Instead of immediately giving your child the answer, ask "What do you notice?" or "What do you think comes next?" Letting them explain their thinking makes pattern practice more meaningful.
