What age are these 2D shape sorting activities for?

Best for preschoolers ages 4 to 5 who already know the basic shapes. Six shape types is a lot — it's meant as a capstone challenge.

Are these 2D shape sorting activities really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

What does 2D mean?

2D means two-dimensional — flat, like a drawing on paper. It has length and width but no depth. You can tell your child: ‘If it's flat like paper, it's 2D!’
