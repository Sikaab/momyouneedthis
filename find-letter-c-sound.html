🔊 Know The C Sound Before You Hunt

You are hunting the hard c sound: /k/, made at the back of the throat like a tiny cough. It is the sound at the start of cat, car and cake — and also cow, cup, cookie and cap.

The way to check any picture is to say its name slowly and listen to the very first sound. If the first sound is the little cough, circle it. If the word starts with any other sound — a hiss, a hum, a pop — it is a trickster, however tempting it looks.

One secret for grown-ups: c has a second, soft sound — /s/ — in words like city and circle. This page hunts only the hard sound. Once the cough is easy to hear, the soft-c words become a fun next case for another day.
