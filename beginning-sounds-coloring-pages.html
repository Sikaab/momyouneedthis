🔍 Stretch The First Sound

As you color, stretch out the beginning sound: “bbbbb-ear.” Exaggerating the first sound helps children isolate it — the exact skill behind phonemic awareness. Then let them try: “what sound does ball start with?”

💡 Why Beginning Sounds Come Before Reading

Children who can hear and identify first sounds learn letter-sound relationships far more easily. This phonemic awareness is one of the strongest predictors of early reading success — and coloring pages make the practice feel like play.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are beginning sounds?

The first sound in a spoken word — the “buh” in ball, the “sss” in sun. Recognizing beginning sounds (phonemic awareness) is a key pre-reading skill, and this coloring page practices it with the letter S.
