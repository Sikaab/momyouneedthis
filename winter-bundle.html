Winter Words and Skills in This Bundle

This bundle is winter through and through — snow, ice and cold-weather play on every page. Two colouring pages (a snowman and a penguin) and a snowflake mandala bring the season's pictures; mitten matching pairs up winter clothes; counting snowflakes and tracing penguin trails build early maths and pencil skills.

A snowman maze, a finish-the-snowflake drawing page, a polar bear colour-by-number, a hot cocoa sequencing page and a winter-homes matching game round out the set — even the sorting page asks what belongs to winter and what belongs to summer.

Can I print one page at a time?

Yes. Every activity has its own PDF download, so you can print a single page now and come back for the rest later.

It is made for toddlers and preschoolers. Younger toddlers enjoy the colouring pages with help, while preschoolers can tackle the counting, tracing and maze pages more independently.

Is the winter bundle free?

Yes — all twelve printables are 100% free to download and print at home. No signup and no payment needed.
