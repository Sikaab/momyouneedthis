○ All About the Circle

Children learn that a circle is perfectly round with no corners and no straight sides while tracing big circles and spotting round things like wheels, the sun, and cookies.

Tracing big circles is often a child's very first drawing win — the line comes back to where it started, and suddenly they made a shape on purpose.

🔍 Circle Hunt Around Your Home

Turn learning into a game! Walk around your home together and spot things shaped like a circle — wheels, clocks, plates, and cookies. Count how many you can find and cheer for each one!

💡 Why Learn About the Circle?

The circle is often the first shape children draw, making it a wonderful starting point for pre-writing skills. Tracing circles builds the smooth, controlled pencil movements children need before they can write letters and numbers.
