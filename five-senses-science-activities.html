🔍 Ask “What” Before “Why”

Preschool science is about noticing, not explaining. Ask “what did you hear?” rather than “why do you think that happened?” Celebrate observations — “you noticed the bell was louder up close!” — and let the wondering come naturally over time.

💡 Why Sensory Science Suits Preschoolers

The five senses are the original science tools — observing, comparing, and describing are exactly what scientists do. Sensory experiments give young children authentic practice with the science process using nothing more exotic than their own bodies and a curious question.
