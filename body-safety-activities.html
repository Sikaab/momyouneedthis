Every child deserves to know their body belongs to them. These printable activities introduce body safety in calm, simple language — empowering young children with words and rules made for ages 2 to 5.

💛 Learn About Body Safety

Children learn the core messages: my body belongs to me, I can say no to unwanted touch (even tickles or hugs), the difference between safe touch (a high-five, a doctor’s check with a parent present) and unsafe touch, and the most important rule — tell a trusted grown-up, and keep telling until someone listens. Scenarios stay simple and non-graphic.

🔍 Use Correct Language Early

Teaching proper names for body parts is itself a safety tool — children who can name things clearly can describe problems clearly. Keep your tone as matter-of-fact as when naming elbows and knees; your comfort teaches them this is a normal, safe topic.

💡 Why The “Keep Telling” Rule Matters

The single most protective message is: tell a trusted grown-up, and if they don’t listen, tell another one. Repeating this — and responding calmly whenever your child brings you worries — teaches them that speaking up works, which is exactly what keeps kids safe.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is body safety for preschoolers?

Age-appropriate lessons that their body belongs to them, they can say no to unwanted touch, and they should always tell a trusted grown-up about anything confusing or uncomfortable — and keep telling until someone listens.

Are these printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

How do I start this conversation?

Keep it short, calm, and routine — like any other safety topic. Use the printable’s scenarios as a script, follow your child’s lead, and revisit the ideas now and then. Brief and repeated beats one big talk.
