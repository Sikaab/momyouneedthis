❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What age can start learning to tell time?

Most children begin grasping o’clock and half-hour times around ages 5 to 6, but coloring clock faces is fun earlier too. These pages are designed to be enjoyable from age 4 up, with learning layered in gently.

Are these coloring pages really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

Should I teach digital or analog first?

Analog first is the classic path — it teaches how time actually works as a cycle. These pages lead with big analog clocks and add simple digital matching as a bonus.
