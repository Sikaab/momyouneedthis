🧠 Why Teach Safety Early?

Young children are constantly learning how to move through the world. Simple conversations and activities can help them begin recognizing safe choices, important rules and trusted adults.

Safety learning doesn't need to feel scary. Keep explanations simple, age-appropriate and focused on what children can do to stay safe.

💛 Keep Safety Learning Simple

Use these activities as opportunities to talk, ask questions and practise safe choices together. Repetition can help important ideas become more familiar over time.
