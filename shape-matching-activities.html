Step by step

🔷 How To Use This Printable

  1. Download and print the shape pages.
  2. Name each shape out loud together as your child looks at it.
  3. Color, trace, or match the shapes at a relaxed pace.
  4. Go on a shape hunt around the house to spot real-life shapes.
