🔊 The F Sound

The F sound is made with the top teeth resting lightly on the bottom lip while air blows through: /f/. It is a wind sound — one of the few sounds you can hold for as long as your breath lasts: ffffish, ffffrog.

Capital F begins Fish, Frog and Flower on this page. You will also meet it standing at the front of names like Finn and Flora and on flags and signs — capitals do the starting job everywhere.

F also begins fox, flag, fan and fork — good words for a spotting game after the printable. And its shape is worth a close look: a straight line with two bars is F; add a third bar at the bottom and it turns into an E.
