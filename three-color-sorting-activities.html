🔍 Name Each Decision

Have your child say the color before placing each object: blue goes here. Verbalizing the decision slows down impulsive sorting and builds accuracy.

💡 Why Three Colors?

Three groups hit the sweet spot for beginners — two is too easy to teach real decision-making, while four or more can overwhelm young sorters.
