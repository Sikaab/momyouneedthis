🧠 Why Learn About Weather?

Learning about weather helps children notice changes in the world around them. Talking about sunshine, rain, clouds, temperature and seasons builds observation skills, vocabulary, curiosity and an early understanding of the natural world.

🌦️ Start With The Seasons

Begin by helping your child notice how the world changes throughout the year. Talk about what people wear, what the trees look like and what kind of weather you see during each season.

🎁 Start The Seasons Activity

💡 Look Outside Together

You don't need a formal lesson to teach weather. Look out the window, talk about the clouds, feel whether the air is warm or cool and ask your child what they notice. Everyday observations can become simple science learning moments.
