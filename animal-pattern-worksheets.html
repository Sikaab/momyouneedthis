🧠 Why Practise Animal Patterns?

Animal patterns help children notice similarities, differences, order and repetition. These playful activities also encourage prediction, visual thinking, memory and early problem-solving skills.

🐾 Start With A Simple Animal Pattern

Begin with an easy repeating pattern. Ask your child to look at the animals, say the pattern out loud and decide which animal should come next.

🎁 Start AB Animal Pattern Activity

💡 Let Your Child Explain Their Thinking

Instead of immediately telling your child which animal comes next, ask "What do you notice?" or "What do you think comes next?" Let them explain their choice and discover the pattern themselves.
