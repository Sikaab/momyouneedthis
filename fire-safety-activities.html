🔍 Practice Your Family Drill

Use the printable’s checklist to walk your escape route together, choose an outdoor meeting spot, and practice getting low under pretend smoke. Doing it once as a game means everyone knows what to do — and kids feel proud, not frightened.

💡 Why Practice Matters More Than Lectures

In an emergency, people do what they have practiced — not what they were told once. A playful drill builds muscle memory for getting low and getting out, and it teaches kids that firefighters in gear are helpers, which matters if they ever need rescue.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What fire safety should preschoolers know?

That firefighters are helpers, how to stop-drop-and-roll, to get low under smoke, and where the family meeting spot is. These activities introduce each idea gently through coloring, games, and a picture drill checklist.

Are these printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

How do I teach this without frightening my child?

Keep it playful and brief. Frame firefighters as community helpers, practice stop-drop-and-roll like a movement game, and do the drill walk-through cheerfully. If your child seems worried, pause and revisit another day.
