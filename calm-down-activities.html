Big feelings are normal — and kids need tools to handle them. These printable calm-down activities teach gentle, age-appropriate strategies like deep breathing and quiet choices that help little ones settle.

🌈 Learn Calm Down Strategies

Children practice “balloon breathing” (slow breaths in and out), choose from a calm-down menu — hug a stuffed animal, sip water, draw the feeling, sit in a cozy corner — and trace a finger labyrinth to slow down. The sheet is designed to be used before, during, or after upset moments.

🔍 Build A Calm Down Corner

Pick a cozy spot with a pillow, a stuffed animal, and the printed calm-down cards. When your child is upset, gently offer the corner instead of demanding calm. Over time, many kids start going there on their own — a huge win for self-regulation.

💡 Why Calm Down Tools Matter

Children are not born knowing how to manage frustration, anger, or overwhelm — they learn it through practice and patient modeling. Giving them concrete strategies, instead of just saying “calm down,” teaches real emotional skills they will use for life.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What are calm down activities for preschoolers?

Simple, soothing strategies young children can use when upset — slow breathing, hugging a comfort object, drawing feelings, sipping water, or sitting in a cozy spot. This printable turns those ideas into cards and exercises kids can practice.

Are these printables really free?

Yes — every printable on MomYouNeedThis is 100% free to download and print. No signup, no payment, just tap the download button.

When should I introduce calm down strategies?

During a calm, happy moment — not in the middle of a meltdown. Practice balloon breathing as a game first, post the choice cards visibly, and gently point to them when big feelings show up.
