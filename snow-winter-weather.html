❄️ Winter Activities for Toddlers and Preschoolers

These winter activities turn cold days into cozy learning time. Snow, mittens, hot cocoa, and frosty animals give toddlers so much to talk about, point at, and color. Our winter activities are all free printables, so you can grab one whenever cabin fever strikes.

The best winter activities mix a little movement with a little quiet focus. Start with a coloring page or matching game at the table, then head outside to spot real snow, footprints, and icicles. Back inside, the same winter activities become a calm-down routine — color, chat, and warm up together.

Tip: keep a small stack of winter activities printed and ready in a folder by the door. When the weather turns, you have instant entertainment with zero prep — and zero screens.

Snow Day Activities With No Printer

Some winter fun needs no printing at all. When the snow flies, try these together:

  • Catch snowflakes on dark paper. Chill a piece of dark paper or fabric outside, then catch flakes on it and look quickly — no two snowflakes are alike.
  • Spot footprints. Fresh snow keeps a record: look for bird tracks, boot prints and paw prints, and guess who walked there first.
  • Count with hot cocoa. Count marshmallows into the mug, count spoonfuls of cocoa, and count the minutes while it cools.
  • Watch window frost. On a cold morning, find the frosty patterns on the glass and trace the feathery shapes with a finger.
